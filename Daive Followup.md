D.A.I.V.E. Follow-Up System — Full Simplified Scope (No Code Version)
Aligned with your requirement: ONLY two lead sources → DAIVE Conversation + Manual CRM Entry
1. Lead Generation Sources (Strict Rule)
1. D.A.I.V.E. AI Conversation (Primary Source)

Any customer conversation on website, WhatsApp, SMS, Messenger, or in-store AI interface.

AI captures intent (e.g., “I’m interested in a car” → LEAD CREATED).

Lead immediately enters the appropriate follow-up flow.

2. Manual Entry From CRM (Secondary Source)

Salesperson adds walk-in, phone call, referral, or returning customer.

Once added, DAIVE automatically identifies stage and starts follow-up.

No Other Sources Allowed

No web forms

No marketing landing pages

No external lead vendors

No file imports

No API feeds from marketplaces

This keeps the entire pipeline clean and controlled.

2. Follow-Up Categories (What DAIVE Must Handle)

D.A.I.V.E. must keep customers engaged across the entire lifecycle, automatically.

A. Lead Nurture (New Leads)

For new leads from DAIVE AI conversation or manual CRM entry:

Immediate message

Daily reminders

Objection handling

Value-based messaging

Price/inventory information

Appointment scheduling

Goal: Convert interest → visit → sale.

B. Visit But No Purchase (Unsold Follow-Up)

For customers who came in but didn’t buy:

Same-day message

48-hour check-in

Comparison/promo

Urgency messaging

Incentives and offers

Re-engagement weekly for a month

Monthly follow-up afterward

Goal: Bring them back.

C. Post-Purchase (Sold Customers)

After a customer buys:

Thank-you message

Care / setup help

Review request

Referral request

Service reminders

Loyalty engagement

Future upgrade offers

Goal: Turn buyers into loyal long-term customers.

D. Service Customer Follow-Up

For customers using dealership service:

Appointment reminders

Vehicle ready notifications

Missed appointment recovery

Future maintenance reminders

Seasonal service suggestions

Recall notices

Goal: Increase service retention.

E. At-Risk Customer Follow-Up (Low Engagement or Low Usage)

Triggered when:

Low interaction detected

Not responding

Decline in activity

Negative sentiment detected

DAIVE sends:

"Need help?"

"We noticed inactivity"

Offer training

Offer solutions

Provide incentives

Goal: Recover the customer before churn.

F. Churn Prevention (Cancellation Flow)

When customer wants to cancel:

Awareness message (“Before you go…”)

Offer help

Offer discount

Offer pause

Phone call from manager

Last retention message

Win-back every 90 days

Goal: Reduce cancellations significantly.

G. Long-Term Loyalty Engagement

DAIVE stays in long-term contact:

Holiday greetings

Birthday messages

Service due reminders

New inventory alerts

Trade-in value suggestions

Owner tips

Anniversary messages

Goal: Maintain relationship for years.

4. Follow-Up Behavior Rules (How DAIVE Must Act)
1. Always Instant

DAIVE must respond instantly in all chats.

2. Always Follow Up Until Customer Opts Out

Persistence is mandatory.

3. Personalize Every Message

Use:

Name

Vehicle of interest

Conversation history

Tone of previous replies

Timing preferences

4. Always Include a Call-To-Action

Every message must drive an action:

“Want to schedule?”

“Ready to come in?”

“Should I hold it for you?”

5. Adjust to Customer Mood

Friendly → urgent → helpful → soft, depending on sentiment.

6. Avoid Spam

Follow the cadence rules to avoid overwhelming users.

7. DAIVE Should Never Leave a Dead-End Message

Must always continue the flow unless the customer stops it.
